A practical Canadian planning framework

What is community economic development planning?

Community economic development planning is a locally driven process for understanding a community's strengths, challenges and opportunities, then turning that knowledge into realistic priorities, fundable projects and measurable action.

This system gives your organization a structured place to collect information, involve stakeholders, assess opportunities, make decisions and document the reasoning behind those decisions. It replaces disconnected notes and blank-page planning with one guided process.

It is built for the realities of Canadian community work: limited staff capacity, volunteer boards, multiple partners, changing funding programs and the need to show clear outcomes.

The result: a documented community economic development process that can support strategic decisions, funding applications, implementation meetings and progress reporting.

A strong CED plan should help you:

  • Understand whether the community is ready to move forward
  • Identify local assets, gaps and emerging opportunities
  • Bring community and organizational perspectives together
  • Choose projects using clear, consistent criteria
  • Connect priorities to realistic funding opportunities
  • Assign responsibility, timelines and measures of progress

The guided process

Five phases from readiness to results

Each phase builds the information and decisions needed for the next, creating a clear path from early discussion to accountable implementation.

Discover

Assess readiness, clarify the purpose and identify who needs to participate.

03

Set priorities, compare project ideas and define practical goals and actions.

04

Assess funding readiness and connect priority projects with Canadian funding streams.

05

Implement & Evaluate

Assign responsibilities, track activity and report progress using consistent measures.

Frequently asked questions

Before you purchase

Who is the Community Economic Development Planning System for?

It is designed for Canadian municipalities, Indigenous organizations, nonprofits, regional development agencies, Community Futures organizations, economic development offices, planning committees and community development professionals.

What format will I receive?

You will receive a 48-page fillable PDF with 274 interactive fields. It can be completed digitally in a modern PDF reader or printed for planning meetings and workshops.

What is the difference between the individual and team licences?

The individual licence is for one user. The Professional Team Licence allows up to 10 people within one organization to use the system for internal planning work.

Does it include funding information?

Yes. It includes federal, provincial and Indigenous community economic development funding streams. Funding programs can change, so confirm current eligibility, deadlines and requirements directly with the funder before applying.

Is this a completed community plan?

No. It is a guided planning system that helps your community or organization develop its own evidence, priorities, projects and implementation plan. It does not replace local decision-making or professional legal, financial or regulatory advice.

How will I receive the product?

Your download will be available immediately after purchase and through the email sent to the address used at checkout.

Can I review a sample first?

Yes. The free sample includes the Community Readiness Assessment section, a worked example, facilitator notes and fillable fields.
