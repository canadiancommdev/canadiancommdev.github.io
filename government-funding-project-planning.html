A practical Canadian planning framework

What makes a project funding-ready?

A funding-ready project is more than a good idea. It clearly explains the need, who will benefit, what will happen, who is responsible, how much it will cost and how results will be measured.

This system brings those pieces together before you begin copying answers into an application portal. It helps your organization build one strong project foundation that can be adapted to different Canadian funders and programs.

It is designed for real planning conditions: limited staff capacity, multiple partners, volunteer boards, tight deadlines and the need to manage several opportunities at once.

The result: a documented project plan that supports stronger grant narratives, clearer budgets, partner discussions, application tracking and implementation follow-through.

A strong funding plan should help you:

  • Demonstrate a clear and evidence-based need
  • Define realistic activities, outcomes and timelines
  • Build a defensible budget and identify contributions
  • Coordinate partners, consultation and approvals
  • Match the project to suitable Canadian funding programs
  • Track applications, decisions and next steps

Frequently asked questions

Before you purchase

Who is this planning system for?

It is designed for Canadian nonprofits, municipalities, Indigenous organizations, community groups, regional development agencies, tourism organizations, boards, committees and private businesses preparing government funding applications.

Does it guarantee funding approval?

No. No planning system can guarantee approval. It helps you organize the information, evidence and project details funders commonly expect, while final decisions remain with each funding organization.

What format will I receive?

You will receive a 49-page fillable PDF with 229 interactive fields. It can be completed digitally or printed for meetings and planning sessions.

What is the difference between the individual and team licences?

The individual licence is for one user. The Team Licence allows up to 10 people within one organization to use the system for internal work.

Does it include Canadian funding information?

Yes. It includes federal programs, regional development agencies, provincial and territorial resources, and Indigenous-specific streams. Always confirm current program details directly with the funder.

How will I receive the product?

Your download will be available immediately after purchase and through the email sent to the address used at checkout.

Can I review a sample first?

Yes. The free sample includes the Project Concept Summary, guidance, funder-perspective notes and fillable fields.
