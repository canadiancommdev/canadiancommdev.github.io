Why tourism businesses require specialized startup planning

A tourism idea must work as both a business and a visitor experience. It may also involve seasonality, property, safety, insurance and local regulatory requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Before you purchase

Who is the Tourism Business Startup System for?

It is designed for Canadian entrepreneurs planning accommodations, tours, experiences, attractions, outdoor activities, cultural tourism, food tourism and other visitor-focused businesses.

Does it tell me exactly which permits I need?

It helps you identify and organize likely permit, licence, zoning, inspection and registration requirements. Exact requirements vary by location and business model, so confirm them with the appropriate municipal, provincial, territorial and federal authorities.

What format will I receive?

You will receive a 25-page fillable PDF with 102 interactive fields. It can be completed digitally or printed.

Does it include financial planning?

Yes. It includes seasonal financial projections, startup and operating considerations, funding resources and a 30/60/90-day launch framework.

Does it include Canadian funding information?

Yes. It includes federal, provincial, territorial and Community Futures resources relevant to tourism businesses. Confirm current program details directly with each funder.

Is a team licence available?

No. This product is currently offered as an individual licence for one entrepreneur or business owner.

How will I receive the product?

Your download will be available immediately after purchase and through the email sent to the address used at checkout.
