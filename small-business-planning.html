Frequently asked questions

Before you purchase

Who is the Small Business Success Planning System for?

It is designed for Canadian entrepreneurs, startups, existing small businesses, Community Futures clients, business associations, chambers of commerce, economic development organizations and business advisors.

Will it create the business plan for me?

It provides the structure, guidance, fillable fields and financial framework. You supply the facts, assumptions and decisions specific to your business.

What financial statements are included?

The system includes an income statement, cash-flow projection and balance sheet, along with break-even, sensitivity and financial-ratio analysis.

What is the difference between the individual and team licences?

The individual licence is for one user. The Team Licence allows up to 10 people within one organization to use it, including organizations supporting business clients.

Does it include Canadian government funding information?

Yes. It includes federal, provincial, territorial and Community Futures resources. Confirm current program details directly with each funder before applying.

How will I receive the product?

Your download will be available immediately after purchase and through the email sent to the address used at checkout.

Can I use the plan with a bank or funder?

The system is designed around the information commonly requested by lenders and funders, but each organization may require additional forms, evidence or professional review.
