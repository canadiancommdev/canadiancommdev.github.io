A professional Canadian planning system

Why community tourism needs more than a list of ideas

Communities often have strong tourism assets but no shared process for deciding how those assets should support local priorities, visitor experiences and realistic action.

This professional planning system gives coordinators, committees and organizations one guided structure for developing a community-led tourism direction without creating every workshop document and planning tool themselves.

It is built for Canadian communities that want tourism development to reflect local identity, capacity and long-term benefit.

The result: a professionally organized tourism planning process that helps people reach agreement and move promising opportunities toward action.

The system helps your group:

  • Move beyond scattered tourism ideas
  • Keep consultation and planning discussions focused
  • Document opportunities and priorities professionally
  • Connect tourism planning with funding and action

Frequently asked questions

Before you purchase

Who is the Community Tourism Planning System for?

It is designed for Canadian municipalities, Indigenous organizations, tourism committees, destination organizations, nonprofits, regional tourism associations, boards and community development teams.

Is this only for established tourism destinations?

No. It can be used by communities beginning tourism planning, refreshing an existing strategy or organizing several tourism ideas into a coordinated action plan.

What format will I receive?

You will receive a 36-page fillable PDF with 28 interactive fields. It can be completed digitally or printed for workshops and planning meetings.

What is the difference between the individual and team licences?

The individual licence is for one user. The Team Licence allows up to 10 people within one organization to use the system for internal tourism planning.

Does it include tourism funding information?

Yes. It includes Canadian federal, provincial, territorial and regional tourism funding resources. Confirm current eligibility, deadlines and requirements directly with each funder.

How will I receive the product?

Your download will be available immediately after purchase and through the email sent to the address used at checkout.

Can I review a sample first?

Yes. The free sample includes Community Asset Mapping tables, facilitator guidance, a worked example and fillable fields.
