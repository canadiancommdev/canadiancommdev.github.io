Built from the work Canadian organizations actually have to do

These planning systems were developed after years of work in community economic development and tourism across Atlantic Canada — including securing multi-governmental funding, facilitating community consultations and developing long-term community plans.

The right tools didn't exist. Every grant application, stakeholder consultation, strategic planning process, or funding proposal meant rebuilding documents, templates, and frameworks from scratch.

Canadian Community Development was created to change that. Our resources are built from real-world experience and designed specifically for Canadian municipalities, community organizations, tourism operators, and economic development officers. Every template, checklist, and planning framework is designed to help organizations save time, strengthen projects, and move from ideas to implementation more efficiently.

Whether you're preparing a funding application, launching a community initiative, developing a tourism strategy, or planning for long-term growth, our goal is to provide practical tools that help you spend less time creating documents and more time advancing projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you choose a resource

Who are these planning systems designed for?

They are designed for Canadian nonprofits, municipalities, Indigenous organizations, tourism groups, regional development agencies, Community Futures offices, business associations, consultants and community leaders.

Are the templates specific to Canada?

Yes. The funding references, examples, planning context and directories are developed for Canadian organizations and include federal, provincial, territorial and selected Indigenous funding streams.

What format will I receive?

Planning Systems are delivered as downloadable, fillable PDF files. You can type into them electronically, save your work and print copies for meetings or planning sessions.

Can my board, staff team or clients use the same copy?

Individual purchases are licensed to one user. Organizational licences are available for up to 10 users within one organization.

Can I see a sample before purchasing?

Yes. Free sample sections are available for the Government Funding & Project Planning System, Community Tourism Planning System and Community Economic Development Planning System.
