Why these tools exist

After years working in community economic development and tourism across Atlantic Canada — securing multi-governmental funding, facilitating community consultations, and developing 5-year community plans — we kept running into the same problem.

The right tools didn't exist. Every grant application, every stakeholder consultation, every planning process meant starting from scratch.

Canadian Community Development was built to change that. Every template, checklist, and planning framework in our series reflects hard-won, real-world experience.
