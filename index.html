Why these tools exist

After years working in community economic development and tourism across Atlantic Canada — securing multi-governmental funding, facilitating community consultations, and developing long-term community plans — we kept running into the same challenge.

The right tools didn't exist. Every grant application, stakeholder consultation, strategic planning process, or funding proposal meant rebuilding documents, templates, and frameworks from scratch.

Canadian Community Development was created to change that. Our resources are built from real-world experience and designed specifically for Canadian municipalities, community organizations, tourism operators, and economic development officers. Every template, checklist, and planning framework is designed to help organizations save time, strengthen projects, and move from ideas to implementation more efficiently.

Whether you're preparing a funding application, launching a community initiative, developing a tourism strategy, or planning for long-term growth, our goal is to provide practical tools that help you spend less time creating documents and more time advancing projects.
